## Acknowledgements & License

### Original hardware

Steve Rutt and Bill Etra designed the original Rutt/Etra Video Synthesizer at Rutt Electrophysics, New York, in 1973. The control set documented in the **Synthesis** and **Reference** sections of this manual is sourced from the Rutt/Etra Video Synthesizer brochure preserved in the [Vasulka archive](https://vasulka.org/archive/Artists5/Rutt-E/VidSynthCont.pdf). The instrument was central to the work of Nam June Paik, Steina and Woody Vasulka, Bill Viola, Gary Hill, and many others. This software is built in the spirit of that lineage.
